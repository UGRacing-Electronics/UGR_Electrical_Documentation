\section{Introduction}
This is the UGR PCB order preparation and submission guide. This document contains all the information necessary for you to submit your PCB design as an electrical engineer on the team to be manufactured, and parts ordered. As of February 2026 this is a new process as new software is used, and new inventory systems implemented.

\section{Prerequisites}
To proceed with submitting your design you will need:
\begin{itemize}
    \item A PC with Altium installed
    \item A PartsBox account (see \cref{create_pb_account})
    \item An internet connection
\end{itemize}

\section{Creating a PartsBox account}
If you already have an account set up, proceed to \cref{prepare}.\\

To set up a PartsBox account, head to \href{https://partsbox.com/}{https://partsbox.com/} and click "Sign Up". Please use your university email and set your handle/nickname to your name. e.g. "YukiSuter".\\

Once you've successfully created an account, send your account details (handle and email address) to your team head, who will add you to the UGRacing organisation.
